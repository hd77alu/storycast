Why StoryCast

The Power of Storytelling

This is a web platform that displays powerful stories from numerous organizations covering climate change in Africa.

All videos are embedded directly from YouTube and remain the property of their original creators: NBC News, ABC News, Channels Television, and Switch TV.

The design emphasizes accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA compliance), responsive layouts, container queries, and semantic markup. No content is hosted on this platform - all media is streamed from YouTube.

Access

Designed for Everyone

Accessibility is not a feature layer. It is a design constraint applied from the first line of markup.

Audio

Audio files for all the videos are available and fully accessible for all users.

Videos

All videos are embedded directly from YouTube. No content is downloaded or hosted on this platform.

Keyboard Navigation

The entire site is navigable by keyboard. Audio players respond to arrow keys for scrubbing, space to play and pause.

Transcripts

Stories are published with full transcripts including timestamps for accessibility.

Inclusive Design

Interactive elements with semantic structure, designed to meet WCAG AA contrast throughout.

Screen Readers

ARIA landmarks, live regions, and descriptive labels are applied throughout.
