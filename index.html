Scorched: East Africa's Climate Crisis

East Africa is ground zero for climate change. Community leaders in northern Kenya tell NBC News Chief International Correspondent Keir Simmons that fertile lands just a decade ago now look like desert. Conflict around the world is also driving food insecurity. “Scorched: East Africa’s Climate Crisis” looks at the global forces at play and asks how we can address these issues. Video by NBC News.
